The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask is my favorite game that I have ever played.

Some of my favorite things about the game is:

    The concept of time and how it rushes you.
    The music made for the game and how that interacts with the world.
    The characters.
    The art style and general vibe you get from the game.
    The story from start to finish.
